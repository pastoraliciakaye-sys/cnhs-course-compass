👋 Welcome!

Choosing a college course can be challenging. CNHS Course Compass helps Senior High School students explore possible courses based on their interests, strengths, passions, and preferences.

Answer the questions below and discover possible academic and career paths that may match you.

📝 Course Finder Quiz

Answer honestly. There are no right or wrong answers.

1. What activity do you enjoy most?

2. Which subject interests you most?

3. What strength describes you best?

4. What type of work sounds interesting?

5. What are you most passionate about?

6. What kind of achievement would make you happiest?

🎯 Your Possible Courses

Remember: A recommendation is only a starting point. Your final college decision should also consider your goals, interests, abilities, finances, school options, and personal circumstances.

💡 College Advice

1. Know Yourself

Consider your interests, strengths, values, personality, and activities that make you feel motivated.

2. Do Not Choose Because of Pressure

Your friends or other people may influence you, but your decision should also reflect your own goals and interests.

3. Research Before Choosing

Check the subjects, admission requirements, possible careers, costs, and schools offering the course.

4. Think About Your Future

Ask yourself what type of work, environment, and lifestyle you may want in the future.

5. Talk to People

Ask teachers, parents, counselors, college students, graduates, or professionals about their experiences.

6. It Is Okay Not to Know Yet

You do not need to have your entire future figured out. Exploring different possibilities is already progress.

7. Passion and Practicality Can Coexist

You can consider both what you enjoy and what opportunities may be available. Finding a balance can help you make an informed choice.

🧭 Before Choosing a Course

Ask yourself these questions:

❤️ Do I enjoy this field?
Would I still be interested in learning about it over time?
🧠 Am I willing to learn its difficult parts?
Every course has challenging subjects.
🎯 Does it connect with my goals?
Think about the kind of future and work you want to pursue.
💰 Have I researched the practical side?
Consider tuition, transportation, materials, and other possible expenses.
📖 Have I researched the actual course?
Look beyond the course title and learn what students actually study.

📖 About the Project

CNHS Course Compass is a Senior High School research prototype designed to help students explore possible college courses based on interests, strengths, passions, and preferences.

🎓 Purpose

The website provides an interactive way for students to begin exploring possible academic and career paths.

📊 Research Prototype

This website was developed as part of an Individual Practical Research 2 project for Senior High School.

⚠️ Important Note

The Course Finder is not a professional psychological, career, or aptitude assessment. Its results are educational suggestions only and should not be treated as a final decision.
